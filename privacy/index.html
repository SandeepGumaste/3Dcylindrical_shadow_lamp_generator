Privacy policy

This is a draft written in plain language for a hobby site run by a private person. It is a starting point and is not legal advice.

Who runs this site

[[OWNER_NAME]], a private person. Contact: [[OWNER_EMAIL]].

What is stored

You can use the preview and the settings without an account. An account is needed to download files. When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account name, email address and profile picture.

For each download we store a record with:

  • the processed picture, shrunk to 512 pixels and turned to grayscale (not your original file);
  • all the settings you used;
  • the version of the app and the time of the download.

The processed picture is kept at the image host imgbb and can be reached by anyone who has its link. Links are not published anywhere, and only your own history lists them.

Why

The records let you see your earlier downloads and load them again in the app. The account name and email identify you and let you sign in. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

How long

Records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. We do not set an automatic expiry. The image host imgbb has its own rules and may remove images; an entry then loses its picture but keeps its settings.

Who sees it

Only you can see your history in the app. The owner can technically reach the database for running and fixing the service. Anyone who has an image link can open that image.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete each history entry in the app. This removes the record and asks imgbb to remove the picture.
  • You can delete your whole account and all its data in the app. This removes your records and your pictures.
  • If something does not work, write to [[OWNER_EMAIL]] and the data will be deleted by hand.

Services that handle data for us

  • Vercel hosts the site and the server code.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores accounts and history records.
  • imgbb stores the processed pictures.
  • Google provides the sign-in.

Each of these has its own privacy policy.

Your choices and contact

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion at any time by writing to [[OWNER_EMAIL]].

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
