How Odd Shadows works, what it stores and how to print the lamp safely.

What is Odd Shadows?

Odd Shadows is a free web app that turns a picture into a 3D printable lamp shade. When a light shines inside, the shade casts the picture as a shadow.

How does it work?

You choose a picture, adjust the settings and watch the preview. Then you download an STL file or an SVG file and print it on your 3D printer.

Which printers and materials can I use?

Any 3D printer that can print an STL file should work. Use a material that handles the heat of your light, and see the safety notes in the terms.

Why do I need to sign in?

The app builds your model on our server and limits how often each person can do that, to keep the free service running. Signing in with Google lets us tell people apart and count how many files each person downloads.

What is stored about me?

Your Google name, email address and profile picture, and a count of the files you download. The picture you pick is sent to our server to build your model and is not saved. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Write to sandeepgumaste10@gmail.com and your account and everything stored about it will be deleted by hand.

Can I use a real flame or only an LED?

Use an LED light. PLA softens at about 60 degrees Celsius, so a real flame can bend or melt the shade and is a fire risk.

Which shapes can I make?

A Tealight shade and a Shadow wall for the table, a Box shadow that casts onto a wall, and a Light source bracket for the wiring.
