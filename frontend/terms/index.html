3D printing and safety

  • Check every model in your slicer before you print.
  • Use the shade with an LED light only, never with fire.
  • PLA softens at about 60 degrees Celsius, so keep the plastic away from anything hot, and stop using the light if it gets warm.
  • Never leave a running printer unattended.
