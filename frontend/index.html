Print a
shadow.

Pick a picture. Odd Shadows turns it into a tealight shade you can print at home. Switch on an LED tealight and the picture appears on the wall as a shadow.

Free to use. Sign in with Google to open the app.

Start making a shade

Three moves.

  1. Pick a picture.

    A photo, a drawing or a logo. Simple shapes with strong contrast cast the cleanest shadows.

  2. Tune the shade.

    Set the size, wall thickness and flame height. The preview lights the shade so you can see the shadow before you print anything.

  3. Download and print.

    Send the STL file to your slicer and print the shade in a material that handles the heat of your light. Put an LED tealight inside.

Good questions.

Can I use fire?

No. Use an LED light only. A printed shade can bend, melt or burn around a flame.

Which printer?

Any 3D printer that can print an STL file. Pick a material that handles the heat of your light.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a Google account. You sign in once to open the app, and there is no password to set.

What is stored?

Your Google name, email and profile picture, so the site knows who you are. The picture you pick is sent to the server to build your model and is not saved.
