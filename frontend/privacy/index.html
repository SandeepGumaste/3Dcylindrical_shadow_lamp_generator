Privacy policy.

Plain language, for a hobby site run by one person. This is a draft and a starting point, not legal advice.

Who runs this site

Sandeep Gumaste, a private person in India. Contact: sandeepgumaste10@gmail.com.

What is stored

You need a Google account to use the app. When you sign in, we receive and store your Google account name, email address and profile picture, the role of your account (user or admin) and the time you first signed in.

Each time you download a file we add one to a counter on your account. We keep the total, the number per kind of lamp and the time of your last download. That is all we record about a download.

We do not store the files you download, the pictures you pick or your settings. Your picture is sent to our server so it can build the model, and it is not saved.

Signing in also sets a session cookie in your browser. It is a sign-in session cookie that keeps you logged in, and nothing else.

Logs and limits

Our hosts, Vercel and Render, keep request logs, which include your IP address and browser (user agent). We also keep a short-lived counter per account that limits how often you can ask for a model, to protect the free service. These counters delete themselves after a few minutes.

Why

Your name and email identify you and let you sign in. The download count shows how the service is used and who uses it heavily, so it stays free and running. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

How long

Your account record and its download counts are kept until you ask us to delete them. We set no automatic expiry. Hosting logs follow the rules of Vercel and Render.

Who sees it

Only the owner can reach the database, for running and fixing the service. Nothing about you is shown to other users.

Deleting your data

  • Write to sandeepgumaste10@gmail.com and your account and everything stored about it will be deleted by hand.
  • Signing out only ends your session in this browser; it does not delete anything.

Services that handle data for us

  • Vercel hosts the website and passes requests to the server.
  • Render runs the server code.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores accounts and download counts.
  • Google provides the sign-in.

Each of these has its own privacy policy, and their servers may be in other countries.

Your choices and contact

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or a deletion at any time by writing to sandeepgumaste10@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
