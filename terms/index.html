3D printing and safety

  • Check every model in your slicer before you print.
  • PLA softens at about 60 degrees Celsius. Do not use PLA close to a real flame or any hot source.
  • Prefer a battery powered LED light and keep it away from the plastic if it gets warm.
  • Never leave a burning candle or a running printer unattended.
