Print a lamp that draws with light

Pick a picture. Odd Shadows turns it into a tealight shade you can print at home. Switch on an LED tealight and the picture appears on the wall as a shadow.

Start making a shade

Free to try in your browser. No account needed to look around.

How it works

  1. Upload a picture

    A photo, a drawing or a logo. Simple shapes with strong contrast cast the cleanest shadows.

  2. Tune the shade and preview the shadow

    Set the size, wall thickness and flame height. The preview lights the shade so you can see the shadow before you print anything.

  3. Download the STL and print

    Send the file to your slicer and print the shade in a material that handles the heat of your light. Put an LED tealight inside.

What you can make

Right now Odd Shadows makes one thing, and aims to make it well: a tealight shade, a printed sleeve that stands over an LED tealight. Use an LED, not a real flame; the FAQ explains why.

  • A portrait of a pet that glows on the bedroom wall.
  • A family crest or a club logo for the shelf.
  • A skyline, a mountain or a favourite constellation.
  • A gift with a photo only the light can show.
