Frequently asked questions

What is Odd Shadows?

Odd Shadows is a free web app that turns a picture into a 3D printable lamp shade. When a light shines inside, the shade casts the picture as a shadow.

How does it work?

You choose a picture, adjust the settings and watch the preview. Then you download an STL file or an SVG file and print it on your 3D printer.

Which printers and materials can I use?

Any 3D printer that can print an STL file should work. Use a material that handles the heat of your light, and see the safety notes in the terms.

Why do I need to log in to download?

The preview and settings work without an account. Logging in with Google lets the site keep a history of your downloads so you can load them again later.

What is stored about me?

Your Google name, email and picture, and for each download a small grayscale copy of the picture, your settings, the app version and the time. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single history entries in the app, or delete your whole account and all its data. You can also write to the contact address in the legal notice.

Can I use a real flame or only an LED?

Use an LED light. PLA softens at about 60 degrees Celsius, so a real flame can bend or melt the shade and is a fire risk.

Why is there only a Tealight shade for now?

The Tealight shade is the first shape that is finished and tested. More shapes may come later.
