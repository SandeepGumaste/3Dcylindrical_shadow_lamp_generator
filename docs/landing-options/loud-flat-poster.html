Print a shadow.

Pick a picture. Odd Shadows turns it into a tealight shade you can print at home. Switch on an LED tealight and the picture appears on the wall.

Start making a shade Free to try in your browser. No account needed to look around.

Three moves.

Pick a picture.

A photo, a drawing or a logo. Simple shapes with strong contrast cast the cleanest shadows.

Tune the shade.

Set the size, wall thickness and flame height. The preview lights it so you see the shadow before you print.

Download and print.

Send the STL to your slicer. Print in a material that handles the heat, then add an LED tealight.

What you can make.

  • A pet that glows.
  • A crest for the shelf.
  • A skyline on the wall.
  • A night sky anywhere.

Right now Odd Shadows makes one thing and aims to make it well: a printed sleeve that stands over an LED tealight.

Good questions.

A real candle?

No. Use an LED tealight only. A printed shade can melt or catch fire around a flame.

Which printer?

Any home FDM printer. PLA works with a cool LED tealight; PETG gives you more margin.

An account?

Not to look around. You sign in when you download a file.

What do you keep?

A small greyscale copy of your picture and your settings, in your history, when you download. Nothing else.
