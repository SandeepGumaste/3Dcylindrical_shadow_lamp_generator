From picture to wall

Upload a picture

A photo, a drawing or a logo. Simple shapes with strong contrast cast the cleanest shadows.

Tune the shade

Set the size, wall thickness and flame height. The preview lights the shade so you see the shadow before you print.

Download and print

Send the STL to your slicer. Print in a material that handles the heat of your light, then add an LED tealight.

Questions you might have

Can I use a real candle?
No. Use an LED tealight only. A printed shade can melt or catch fire around a flame.
What printer and material?
Any home FDM printer. PLA works with a cool LED tealight; PETG gives you more margin.
Do I need an account?
Not to look around. You sign in when you download a file.
What do you keep?
When you download, a small greyscale copy of your picture and your settings go to your history. Nothing else.
