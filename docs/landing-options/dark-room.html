Print a lamp that draws with light

Pick a picture. Odd Shadows turns it into a tealight shade you can print at home. Switch on an LED tealight and the picture appears on the wall.

Start making a shade Free to try in your browser. No account needed to look around.

From picture to wall in three steps

  1. Upload a picture

    A photo, a drawing or a logo. Simple shapes with strong contrast cast the cleanest shadows.

  2. Tune the shade and preview the shadow

    Set the size, wall thickness and flame height. The preview lights the shade so you see the shadow before you print anything.

  3. Download the STL and print

    Send the file to your slicer and print in a material that handles the heat of your light. Put an LED tealight inside.

Questions you might have

Can I use a real candle?

No. Use an LED tealight only. A printed shade can melt or catch fire around a flame.

What printer and material do I need?

Any home FDM printer. PLA works with a cool LED tealight; PETG gives you more margin.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. You sign in when you download a file.

What do you keep?

When you download, the app saves a small greyscale copy of your picture and your settings to your history. Nothing else.
